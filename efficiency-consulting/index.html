Most bookkeepers keep score. We help you change it.

By background I'm IT and operations — nearly 30 years solving operational and financial problems for businesses — and I'm a QuickBooks Online Gold Certified ProAdvisor. In short: I'm an efficiency expert.

That background means we see things most bookkeepers don't. When we look at your financials, we're not just checking whether the numbers reconcile — we're looking at the processes behind them. Where is time being wasted? Where are costs leaking out? Where is the business working harder than it needs to?

We've worked across Finance and Accounting, IT, Operations, and Procurement. That breadth means we can follow a problem wherever it leads — whether it's a broken approval workflow, a vendor relationship that's costing more than it should, or a manual process that should have been automated years ago.

The result is a plain-language findings report with prioritized recommendations you can actually act on — not a 50-page consulting deck that sits on a shelf.

What's included

  • Financial workflow assessment — where time and money are being lost
  • Process gap identification across finance, operations, and procurement
  • Recommendations for automation and tooling (including QuickBooks Online)
  • Vendor and procurement process review
  • Operational friction analysis — what's slowing the business down
  • Plain-language findings report with prioritized recommendations

Who this is for

  • Business owners who feel like they're working harder than the numbers justify
  • Companies that have grown but whose processes haven't kept up
  • Owners who want more than a bookkeeper — they want a thinking partner
  • Anyone who suspects there's a better way to run things but isn't sure where to start

Process

How it works

  1. 01

    Discovery conversation

    We start with a conversation about your business — how it runs, where the friction is, and what you'd change if you could. We ask a lot of questions. That's intentional.

  2. 02

    Process and financial review

    We review your financial data, workflows, and operational setup. We're looking for patterns — where time is being lost, where costs are higher than they should be, where the process breaks down.

  3. 03

    Findings and recommendations

    You get a plain-language report with prioritized recommendations — what to fix first, what the impact is, and how to do it. Practical, actionable, and specific to your business.
