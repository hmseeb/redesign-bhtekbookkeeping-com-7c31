We believe the most important starting point is understanding your business.

With nearly 30 years of experience solving problems across Finance and Accounting, IT, Operations, and Procurement, we bring more than bookkeeping expertise — we bring perspective. That breadth of experience means we understand how the numbers connect to the decisions that actually move a business forward.

Whether your books need to be maintained daily or brought up to date from a backlog, we provide reliable, consistent support to keep everything accurate and current. At the same time, we look beyond the numbers to identify opportunities for greater efficiency and improved processes.

Our goal is simple: deliver clean, timely financials while helping you run your business more effectively.

Cross-functional experience

Most bookkeepers know the numbers. We know the business behind them.

Nearly 30 years working across four functional disciplines gives us a perspective that goes well beyond the ledger.

Finance & Accounting

Deep hands-on experience in financial reporting, general ledger management, budgeting, forecasting, and month-end close processes. We understand what clean books look like — and what it takes to get there.

Information Technology

Exposure to IT operations and systems gives us a practical understanding of how technology supports financial workflows — from accounting software to ERP systems and process automation.

Operations

Operational experience means we see the full picture of how a business runs. We can identify where financial friction originates and recommend process improvements that go beyond the ledger.

Procurement

Procurement and vendor management experience informs how we review purchasing workflows, identify cost inefficiencies, and support better financial controls around spending.

Meet the owner

You work with Jeremy directly.

Jeremy Racicot founded BH Tek Bookkeeping in Houston, Texas. His background is IT and operations, with nearly 30 years solving operational and financial problems for businesses. That background shapes how the firm works. We look at your books the way an operator does, and we care as much about the process behind the numbers as the numbers themselves.

Jeremy is a QuickBooks Online Gold Certified ProAdvisor. He started BH Tek Bookkeeping because small business owners kept telling him the same story. They were spending their evenings on bookkeeping, or they were handing their CPA a mess every spring. Clean books all year solve both problems.

When you work with BH Tek Bookkeeping, you work with Jeremy directly. You can connect with him on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/jeremyracicot.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a free 20-minute call.
