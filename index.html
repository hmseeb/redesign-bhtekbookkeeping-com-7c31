Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What do you need from me to get started?

Every engagement starts with a free 20-minute call. We look at how your books are set up now, talk about what you need, and give you a straight price based on the size and shape of your books. If you are already in QuickBooks Online, we review your file and clean up anything that needs it. If you are not, we set it up correctly from the start.

Do you work directly with my CPA?

Yes. Our bookkeeping work organizes and maintains your financial records — it is not tax preparation or tax advice — so we are glad to coordinate with your CPA and keep your books ready for them, without the usual back-and-forth.

How much does catch-up bookkeeping cost?

It depends on how far back the issues go and the shape your file is in. We start with a short scope call to understand the situation, then give you a straight price based on the size and shape of your books — and we tell you what we would recommend before we start.

What accounting software do you use?

We work exclusively in QuickBooks Online, and we are a Gold Certified ProAdvisor firm — so your file is set up right, maintained right, and ready whenever your CPA needs it. Payroll records are tracked in QuickBooks Online too, so your books and payroll stay in sync.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Houston, Cypress, and Harris County from our office at 12730 Cypress Valley Rd, Cypress, TX 77429. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
